Company Profile

Powering Businesses with Precision and Unmatched Reliability

Pixel Energy Corp is a leading power and energy solutions company based in Coimbatore. Founded by a team of industry veterans with around 60 years of combined experience in power conditioning, we specialize in offering engineered, practical energy solutions.

We provide reliable and practical solutions for power protection, backup power, solar energy, and electrical power management. With a strong focus on quality products, technical expertise, and dependable after-sales support, we help businesses maintain continuous, stable, and efficient power for their critical operations.

We work closely with our customers to understand their specific power requirements, recommend the right solutions, and provide comprehensive support including installation, commissioning, and ongoing maintenance.
